# PROJECT-AVIATION-ACCIDENT-DATABASE-PHASE-1-

## Project Overview

This project aims to explore a complete dataset of aviation accidents from 1962 to 2023, compiled by the National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB). Through this analysis, we will identify risk factors, trends, and suggest aircraft types with minimal risk for our commercial and private aviation expansion.

## Data Understanding

We are working with a CSV version of the Aviation Accident & Synopses named AviationData.csv, which can be found on [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/khsamaha/aviation-accident-database-synopses/data) and was originally from the National Transportation Safety Board that includes aviation accident data from 1962 to 2023 about civil aviation accidents and selected incidents in the United States and international waters. Each record represents an aviation accident/incident. Dataset Size: 88889 entries with 31 columns

## Data Analysis and Visualisation

The objective is to determine the potential risks of aircrafts and to provide recommendations about which aircraft models would be of the lowest risk for the company to invest in.
My findings can be found on my [Tableau dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/authoring/AviationTableauDashboard/Dashboard1#1) and [Slide Presentation](https://www.canva.com/design/DAGe6yQdFJ4/kwBssa0y3uq68VftheginQ/edit?utm_content=DAGe6yQdFJ4&utm_campaign=designshare&utm_medium=link2&utm_source=sharebutton)

## Conclusion

Here are the 3 main recommendations based on my results;
-Prioritize the acquisition of aircrafts from makes with historically lower accident rates
-Consider aircraft types that have 2 or more engines since the accident rate is lower.
-Develop strategies to mitigate risks associated with weather conditions.
